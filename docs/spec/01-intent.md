# 01 — Intent

## Who this is for

**Hila Noah Zablianov**, licensed Israeli CPA (`רואת חשבון`) since 2018, BA in
business administration and accounting from הקריה האקדמית אונו. Solo practice,
fully digital/remote, Holon area. Her existing client base is `עוסק פטור/מורשה`
self-employed people; this site targets a different audience entirely.

Existing assets: a mobile business card at `card.hnz-cpa.com` she likes; a Wix
site at `www.hnz-cpa.com` she dislikes; an active Instagram (`77` posts, `948`
followers) where she already publishes Hebrew tax explainers to camera.

## The visitor

A salaried Israeli employee (`שכיר/ה`), mobile, WhatsApp-native, who has a vague
sense that *"מגיע לי משהו"* and has deferred acting on it for years. They are not
comparing her against another CPA. They are deciding between **acting and not
acting** — and their dominant alternative is continuing to do nothing.

Their real blockers, in order:

1. **Suspicion.** The tax-refund sector is a documented fraud hotspot. The
   archetypal approach is a stranger phoning to promise ₪8,000.
2. **Fear of consequences.** *"ואם אסתבך עם מס הכנסה?"* — almost no competitor
   addresses this honestly, and it is the most answerable fear on the list.
3. **Perceived bureaucracy.** Filing is free via the Tax Authority's own system,
   yet only a small fraction of eligible people do it.
4. **Data anxiety.** Handing `ת.ז.` and `טופס 106` to someone found online.

Price is not in the top four. It becomes relevant only for a visitor who has
already decided to use *someone*, which is a minority of traffic.

## The job of the site

**Primary:** visitor opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. That is the
conversion event.

**Secondary:** visitor completes a 5-question eligibility screening and hands off
to WhatsApp carrying their answers, so Hila opens the conversation already
briefed.

**Tertiary:** visitor leaves a name and phone number for a callback.

**Explicitly not a goal:** educating the visitor to the point where they file
alone. The site will link to the free government route — honestly, and in the
hero of at least one page — because that disclosure is simultaneously the
strongest trust signal available and the best defence against a consumer-law
complaint. Some visitors will take it. That is an acceptable, even desirable,
cost: the ones who leave on that basis were never going to be profitable clients.

## Success criteria

**At launch (week 6):**

- All launch pages indexed; brand queries ranking.
- Google Business Profile live and verified, service-area, address hidden.
- WhatsApp Business configured with the after-hours auto-reply.
- Zero prohibited copy on any page, verified against the checklist in `02`.
- Lighthouse ≥ 95 on performance and accessibility, mobile.
- The year-math module returns correct values for a set of frozen test dates
  including 31.12 and 01.01 rollover.

**90 days:**

- Leads arriving from three distinct sources (GBP, organic long-tail, direct).
- Her median first response under 5 minutes during business hours.
- 10–15 Google reviews accumulated **organically** — note the constraint in
  `02` on soliciting them.
- First-party eligibility-check statistics being logged, for the annual
  original-data piece that is the site's best long-term link asset.

**What failure looks like, stated plainly so we can recognise it:**

- A disciplinary complaint. This is the only outcome that is genuinely
  unrecoverable, and it is why `02` is the most important file in this spec.
- A page that converts by sounding like the refund factories. If the copy could
  have been written by an unlicensed lead-gen company, we have thrown away the
  entire premise.
- Leads she cannot answer within the day. Volume without speed-to-lead is worse
  than no volume: it burns the one advantage a solo practitioner has.

## Non-goals for v1

- English / olim version — phase 2, and as a single page with `hreflang`, never
  a mirrored site.
- A refund-amount calculator that outputs shekels. Prohibited, and it converts a
  marketing asset into an accountability liability.
- Pages for her existing self-employed services beyond one thin page. Mixing two
  funnels on a zero-authority domain dilutes topical focus.
- Paid social. See `02` — push advertising to people who have not expressed
  interest is prohibited, and this is unresolved post-amendment.
- More than one city page at launch.
