## 1. Product overview

- **One line:** a licensed CPA personally checks a salaried employee's last six
  tax years and files the refund claim (`טופס 135`) if there is one.
- **Who sells it:** הילה נח זבליאנוב, `רואת חשבון` since 2018, a solo practice,
  fully remote, based in Holon. Facts and contact details are in `src/content/site.js`.
- **Category:** `החזרי מס לשכירים`. It is an `עיסוק נלווה` (ancillary service),
  not `ראיית חשבון`, and is labelled `שירות נלווה` in the footer and JSON-LD only.
- **Conversion:** the visitor opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message (primary),
  completes the eligibility screening and hands off to WhatsApp with their
  answers (secondary), or calls (tertiary). There is no form backend.

## 2. Ideal customer

A salaried Israeli employee, on mobile and WhatsApp-native, who vaguely feels
`מגיע לי משהו` and has put it off for years. They are deciding **whether to act
at all**, not which CPA to pick. The real competitor is doing nothing.

Blockers, in order (from `docs/spec/01-intent.md`):
1. **Suspicion.** The sector is a fraud hotspot, and the classic approach is a
   stranger phoning to promise ₪8,000.
2. **Fear of consequences:** `ואם אסתבך עם מס הכנסה?`
3. **Perceived bureaucracy.**
4. **Data anxiety** about handing `ת.ז.` and `טופס 106` to a stranger.

Price is not in the top four.

## 3. Positioning

One licensed person does the work herself, signs the report and answers for it
later, and tells you when there is nothing to claim. The site has to **not
sound like the refund factories**. If a line could have come from an
unlicensed lead-gen company, rewrite it.

## 4. What the generic playbooks say that we CANNOT do

| Skill default | Status here | Use instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Testimonials, reviews, star ratings, "join 10,000 customers" | **Prohibited**, even anonymised (ethics decision 14.13.5) | Her photo and video (`2(ד)`), her named byline, her process stated as fact |
| Case studies, average refund, success rate, client count | **Prohibited** (`כלל 17(ג)(7)`) | State Comptroller figures (₪3.6bn, about 2m cases) |
| Any estimated or promised amount, shekel calculators | **Prohibited** | The eligibility screening, which never shows a number |
| Pricing, "free", discounts, "from ₪X", money-back guarantee, "no refund, no fee" | **Prohibited**. The word `חינם` is banned verbatim | `ללא עלות`, `אין דמי פתיחת תיק`, payment timing only |
| "Expert", "leading", "best", any superlative | **Prohibited** | `מתמחה` only |
| Comparison tables, "unlike other companies" | **Prohibited** | Nothing. Describe her own process |
| Popups, exit-intent, slide-ins, timed modals | **Prohibited** (`2(ב)(4)`) | Inline sections and the sticky bar |
| Scarcity, countdown urgency ("only X days left!") | **Prohibited** when dramatised | The deadline as a neutral statement of law, never in the H1 |

## 5. What converts here (allowed levers)

- **The deadline as law:** the year that closes on 31 December, generated by
  `src/lib/refundWindow.js` and never hardcoded.
- **Honesty as a trust asset:** the ~1.4% debt risk shown at full size, the
  gov.il self-filing link, and "I'll tell you if there's nothing".
- **Authority on loan:** State Comptroller figures and statute sections.
- **The fraud inversion:** she does not cold-call, so anyone who called you
  promising a sum is not her.
- **Friction removal:** no documents needed up front, a pre-filled WhatsApp
  message, the client approves before anything is filed, no credit card.
- **Commitment and personal relevance:** the eligibility screening
  (`src/islands/quiz.js`), whose result never states an amount.
- **Implementation intention:** the calendar reminder for "not now".
- **Speed to lead:** under 5 minutes in business hours. Operational, not a feature.

## 6. Voice

Plain, concrete, warm, first person singular (`אני`). Use inclusive `נבדוק` /
`נתחיל` rather than imperatives aimed at the reader. Keep it gender-neutral with
no slash forms (`שכיר/ה`). **No em or en dashes in any visitor-facing string**
(CI fails the build). No decorative arrows. `רו״ח` with gershayim U+05F4.

## 7. Channels and measurement

GA4 is consent-gated and inert until `GA4_MEASUREMENT_ID` is set. Events:
`whatsapp_click`, `call_click`, `reminder_click`, `gov_link_click` and
`quiz_start` / `quiz_step` / `quiz_result_*` / `quiz_to_whatsapp`. Event
parameters never carry answers or personal data. UTM convention:
`utm_source` / `utm_medium` / `utm_campaign`. The WhatsApp message wording
varies per surface (hero, sticky, final, quiz) so she can see where a lead came
from without opening GA4.
